Tips for every part of Writing

Essay · Part 1, compulsory. You'll read two short texts giving different views on one topic. Summarise and evaluate the key points from both, then give your own opinion woven through the essay. 240–280 words, formal/neutral discursive register.

Letter · Part 2, one of three options. At C2 this is almost always formal – to an editor, an organisation, or an official – with a clear purpose stated immediately and a firm, courteous tone throughout. 280–320 words.

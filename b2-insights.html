B2 Item Insights

Anonymous, aggregate answer data from the live B2 First pages. It surfaces items that almost everyone gets wrong (likely mis-keyed or too hard) and multiple-choice items where a wrong option is chosen nearly as often as the key (likely ambiguous). No student names or accounts, just counts. Data is collected from the moment learners start answering, so early on the numbers will be thin.

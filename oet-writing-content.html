Tips for the OET letter-writing task

Writing · one task, 45 minutes (5 min reading + 40 min writing). Read a short case-note extract and write the letter the task asks for (usually a referral, discharge, transfer or letter of advice) to the stated recipient. About 180-200 words. Use only the case notes given, don't invent extra information.

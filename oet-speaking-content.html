Tips for the warm-up and both role-plays

Warm-up · ungraded. A short, informal chat about your professional background before the role-plays begin. It isn't scored, but it sets the tone, answer naturally and specifically rather than with rehearsed one-liners.
    Role-play · two per test, about 5 minutes each with 3 minutes to prepare. You play the healthcare professional (Candidate); a partner or teacher plays the Interlocutor role from the other card. Read only your own card during prep, don't look at the other role's card until after you've practised.
